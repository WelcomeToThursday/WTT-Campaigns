# Trader-price perks

Select and save these personal perks in the perk editor when your campaign permits them.

| Perk | Effect | Points provided |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Personality Vacuum | Purchases from supported base-game traders cost 20% more; Charisma cannot gain experience. Custom traders are excluded. | 2 |
| Third Leg | Therapist purchases cost 5% less; sprint speed is reduced by 1%. | 1 |

Personality Vacuum and Third Leg cannot be combined. Third Leg also conflicts with Sprinter.

## What prices change

Modifiers apply to purchases from the affected traders in both their shops and the flea interface, including currency prices and barter requirements. They do not change selling proceeds, repairs, insurance, clothing-service fees or non-trader flea offers.

Normal stock, loyalty, quest and purchase limits still apply. Other characters keep their ordinary prices.

## Rounding and stale offers

Payment totals round upward after multiplying the requirement by the purchase quantity. A one-item barter increased to 1.2 requires two items for one purchase, or six for five purchases. A 101-rouble price discounted to 95.95 costs 96 roubles for one purchase.

If a price changes while an offer is open, the purchase can be rejected without taking payment. Refresh the offer and check the new total before trying again.

Removing a perk and saving restores ordinary prices after the character reloads.
