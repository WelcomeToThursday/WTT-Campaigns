# Juice Time and Sailor's Nostalgia

Each perk costs two points and grants an additional effect when your living campaign PMC consumes a matching item during a raid.

| Perk | Matching items | Additional effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Juice Time | Apple, Grand, Vita and Russian Army pineapple juice | Painkiller for 60 seconds |
| Sailor's Nostalgia | Canned humpback salmon, herring, Pacific saury and sprats | Health regeneration at 2 HP per second total for 30 seconds |

The item must actually be consumed. Cancelling before any consumption grants no buff. Cancelling after consumption has started does not revoke an effect already applied.

A use grants its effect once. A separate partial use can refresh the timer, but repeated uses do not stack additional duration or regeneration effects.

Sailor's Nostalgia heals one eligible body part at a time, skipping destroyed or fully healed parts. Its rate is **2 HP per second total**, rather than per body part; healing can be lower when a part reaches full health.

These extra effects do not apply to regular PMCs, Scavs, AI or stash/hideout use. Ordinary food effects and [Diet's resource reduction](item-resources.md) still apply. The perks can coexist with [Allergic](allergy-container.md).
