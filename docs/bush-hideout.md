# Bushborne and No FiR for Hideout

## Bushborne

Bushborne costs five points. It reduces bush slowdown and interaction noise to 25% of their normal effect for your campaign PMC.

The slowdown multiplier applies to lost speed. For example, a bush that normally limits movement to 20% of normal speed instead allows 80%. Restrictions on sprinting, jumping and prone movement still apply. Other terrain restrictions, including swamps outside bush triggers, are unaffected.

Bush interaction sound range and volume are reduced. Other characters and bots keep their normal behavior.

## No FiR for Hideout

This common campaign rule allows non-found-in-raid materials to satisfy hideout construction requirements. It does not change the items' found-in-raid flags or remove quest, barter, item-type or other suitability requirements.

The campaign must enable the rule. Server owners maintaining an older bundled configuration can add `69ce5eb3e4b79de94a0d78c8` to `EnabledCommonIds`, restart the server, and save the campaign character's perk selection again. Existing configuration choices are preserved when updating.
