# Medical kits, food and keys

These effects apply to the campaign character with the relevant perk selected. Regular PMC, Scav and AI behavior is unchanged.

| Perk | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Well That Hurt! | Grizzly, AFAK, Salewa, IFAK, Car and AI-2 medical kits consume 25% more resource for healing and injury treatment. Other medical items are unaffected. |
| Diet | Food and drinks use half their normal resource for the requested consumption. |
| Safecracker | A successful mechanical-key use has a 75% chance to consume a use. Normal last-use disposal still applies. |

Well That Hurt! and Diet apply in raids and during stash use. They change resource costs; normal consumption timing, interruption and item disposal still apply. A kit must have enough resource to pay for the requested treatment.

## Rounding during stash use

Stash food costs round to the nearest whole unit, with halfway values rounded to the nearest even number. With Diet, three requested resource units cost two, while one requested unit costs zero. This can leave a one-unit food item in your stash after use.

With Well That Hurt!, less than 1.25 remaining kit resource cannot fund one whole HP of stash healing. Fractional leftovers can remain. Stash and raid resource remainders may differ because the game uses different rounding for each.

For food that also grants temporary raid effects, see [Juice Time and Sailor's Nostalgia](https://github.com/WelcomeToThursday/WTT-Campaigns/wiki/consumables).
