# Battle Pass controls

With a campaign character active, open the campaign banner on the main menu. Browse **Battle Pass**, **Seasonal Rewards** and **About the Season** to view its content.

Use Q/E or the page arrows to browse. Back or Escape returns to the menu. Reward selection is preserved while changing tabs until the hub closes.

## Claim a reward

1. Select a tile to inspect its contents and requirements.
2. Meet its faction, level, quest, previous-page and document requirements.
3. Choose the claim action. If Classified documents are needed to cover a shortage, review and confirm their use.
4. Read the result before continuing. Physical rewards need enough stash space.

The character reloads after a successful transaction, returning to the selected hub tab and page. Closing the hub does not undo a completed claim.

For collection limits and locked rewards, see [documents and rewards](https://github.com/WelcomeToThursday/WTT-Campaigns/wiki/battle-pass-gameplay).

## Exchange documents

Open the exchange window and choose **DOCUMENTS** or **CONTAINER**.

Click owned documents to add them to the exchange; click a selected stack to remove a unit. For a document exchange, also select the document type you want to receive. Review the cost and confirm.

Classified documents cannot be used as exchange sources. Container exchanges are unavailable when the campaign has no eligible crate or contents pool.

## Tutorial

Use the information button beside the campaign badge to replay the eight-step tutorial. It also opens automatically on the first eligible visit.

| Action | Controls |
| --- | --- |
| Next step | NEXT, E, Right Arrow, Space or Enter |
| Previous step | PREVIOUS, Q or Left Arrow |
| Close without marking complete | Escape |
| Remember completion | FINISH or SKIP TUTORIAL |

Clicking a highlighted region advances the tutorial without activating the control underneath. The tutorial covers rewards, requirements, ordinary and Classified documents, collection limits and exchanges. Its completion preference is shared by characters on the same installation and does not change progression.
