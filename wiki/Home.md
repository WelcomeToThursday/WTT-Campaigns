# WTT-Campaigns Wiki

Welcome to the WTT-Campaigns wiki: guides for playing campaigns, understanding perks and rewards, and creating your own stories in SPT.

These guides cover **0.11.0 beta**, targeting **SPT 4.1.x / EFT 0.16.9.40743**. They describe the bundled campaign and supported features; custom campaigns can change perks, rewards and progression.
