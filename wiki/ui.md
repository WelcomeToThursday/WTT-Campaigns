# Menus and perks

## Character selector

Open **CHARACTERS** from the menu or press **F8** outside a raid. Browse the cards to switch characters, create a campaign character, or open its available editing and management actions.

The loading screen names the character being loaded and its campaign. Wait for loading to finish before continuing. See [campaign characters](https://github.com/WelcomeToThursday/WTT-Campaigns/wiki/characters) for creation, deletion and wiping.

## Perks

The **MODIFIERS** tab beside Skills and Mastery shows the active campaign's common rules and your selected personal perks. This is a read-only view; use the character selector's perk editor to change selections when allowed by the campaign.

The editor shows point costs, conflicts and unavailable choices. Review the confirmation before accepting a save. Changes are subject to campaign rules and cannot be made during a raid. See [perk display](https://github.com/WelcomeToThursday/WTT-Campaigns/wiki/skills-reference) and [compatibility](https://github.com/WelcomeToThursday/WTT-Campaigns/wiki/compatibility).

## Campaign hub

With a campaign character active, select the campaign banner on the main menu. The hub provides campaign information, Battle Pass rewards and story access.

Reward claims, document exchanges and progress are local to your character. Read [Battle Pass controls](https://github.com/WelcomeToThursday/WTT-Campaigns/wiki/battle-pass-ui) for navigation and [documents and rewards](https://github.com/WelcomeToThursday/WTT-Campaigns/wiki/battle-pass-gameplay) for costs and limits.

## Story and trader visits

Open **Character → Tasks → Story** to read chapters, objectives and notes. At a supported trader, choose **Visit** beside Buy and Sell to enter the room and start any available conversation.

Campaigns without authored chapters still have an empty Story journal and supported trader visits. See [story and quests](https://github.com/WelcomeToThursday/WTT-Campaigns/wiki/story-system).
