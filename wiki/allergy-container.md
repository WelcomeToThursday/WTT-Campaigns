# Allergic and Broken Secure Container

## Allergic

Allergic provides three points. When first selected, it assigns three distinct food, drink, medicine or stimulant item types from installed content. Using a matching item during a raid applies three distinct symptoms chosen from this list:

| Symptom | Duration | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pain | 30 seconds | Pain |
| Tremor | 20 seconds | Tremor |
| Tunnel vision | 20 seconds | Tunnel vision |
| Health loss | 30 seconds | 3 HP lost per second total |
| Hydration loss | 30 seconds | 3 hydration lost per second |
| Energy loss | 30 seconds | 3 energy lost per second |

Assigned item types persist through saves, character switches and restarts. Removing and reselecting the perk does not reroll them. An inactive perk does not cause symptoms.

Effects apply once per use to your living campaign PMC during raids. Regular PMCs, Scavs, AI and stash/hideout use are unaffected. Health loss affects one eligible body part at a time and can destroy it; repeated rate effects refresh their timer instead of stacking.

If required item templates are missing, they are not silently replaced. Restore the campaign's content dependencies if selection cannot be completed. [Juice Time and Sailor's Nostalgia](consumables.md) can remain selected alongside Allergic.

## Broken Secure Container

Broken Secure Container provides six points and restricts what you can place inside your secure container. Allowed categories include money, keys, special equipment, maps and specific dogtag/container types. The container's ordinary restrictions still apply.

The check includes nested containers and their contents. Placing a prohibited item inside another container does not bypass the rule.

Selecting this perk does not delete, move or confiscate items already stored. You can remove prohibited items, but cannot put them back while the perk is active. Removing the perk restores normal container handling. Other characters' inventories are unaffected.
